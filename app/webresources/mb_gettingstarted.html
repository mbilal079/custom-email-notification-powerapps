Email Notification Hub

Send personalized emails to users, contacts, recipient lists and external addresses, from templates, with your signature.

Administrator setup (one time)

  1. Open Settings and create a record named Default with Active Configuration = Yes.
  2. Choose Send From: the flow connection owner's mailbox, or a Shared Mailbox (enter its address; the connection owner needs Send As permission).
  3. Set Organization Name, an optional Global Footer and Archive BCC Address.
  4. Leave Test Mode on and enter a Test Mode Recipient until you have sent a test. Every email is redirected to that address while Test Mode is on.
  5. In Power Automate → Solutions → Email Notification Hub, make sure the flow Email Hub - Process Email Message is On.
  6. Assign the security roles: Email Hub Administrator, Email Hub Manager or Email Hub Sender (together with Basic User).

Sending an email

  1. Go to Email Messages → New.
  2. Pick Send To (Single User, Single Contact, Recipient List or Email Addresses) and select the recipients.
  3. Optionally pick a Template: the subject and body are filled in for you.
  4. Review the body, choose a Signature, then click Send Now.
  5. Watch Delivery Status move from Queued → Sending → Sent. The Delivery tab lists every recipient.

Use Delivery Mode = Personalized to send one email per recipient with their own name and details. Single email sends one message with everyone on the To line.

Personalization tokens

TokenReplaced with
{{FirstName}} {{LastName}} {{FullName}}Recipient's name
{{Email}}Recipient's email address
{{Company}} {{JobTitle}}Recipient's company / job title (when known)
{{SenderName}} {{SenderEmail}} {{SenderJobTitle}}The person who owns the email message
{{OrganizationName}}Organization Name from Settings
{{Today}}The send date
{{Signature}}Places your signature here; otherwise it is added at the end

Tokens work in both the subject and the body, and are case-sensitive.

Recipient lists

  • Create a Recipient List, then add List Members from its form.
  • A member can be a User, a Contact, a whole Dataverse Team (all members receive it) or an External Email.
  • Tick Opted Out to keep a member on the list without emailing them.
  • Duplicate addresses are sent once.

Signatures

Create your own signature under Signatures and tick My Default. It is pre-selected on every new email.

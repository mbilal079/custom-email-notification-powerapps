Email Notification Hub

Send personalized emails to users, contacts, recipient lists and external addresses, from templates, with your signature.

Administrator setup (one time)

  1. Open Settings and create a record named Default with Active Configuration = Yes.
  2. Choose the Delivery Channel: Office 365 Outlook (no admin setup; send from the connection mailbox or a Shared Mailbox) or Dataverse Email (server-side sync; mailboxes must be approved).
  3. Keep Create Email Activities on so every email appears on the timeline of the recipient and the related record.
  4. Set Organization Name, an optional Global Footer and Archive BCC Address.
  5. On the Email Design tab pick a layout, brand colour and logo, then click Preview Email Design.
  6. Leave Test Mode on and enter a Test Mode Recipient until you have sent a test. Every email is redirected to that address while Test Mode is on.
  7. In Power Automate → Solutions → Email Notification Hub, make sure the flows Email Hub - Send via Outlook and Email Hub - Release Scheduled Messages are On.
  8. Assign the security roles: Email Hub Administrator, Email Hub Manager or Email Hub Sender (together with Basic User).

Sending an email

  1. Go to Email Messages → New.
  2. Pick Send To (Single User, Single Contact, Recipient List or Email Addresses) and select the recipients.
  3. Optionally pick a Template: the subject and body are filled in for you.
  4. Review the body, choose a Signature, then click Send Now.
  5. Watch Delivery Status move from Queued → Sending → Sent. The Delivery Log lists every email, each linked to its Email activity.

Use Delivery Mode = Personalized to send one email per recipient with their own name and details. Single email sends one message with everyone on the To line.

Personalization tokens

TokenReplaced with
{{FirstName}} {{LastName}} {{FullName}}Recipient's name
{{Email}}Recipient's email address
{{Company}} {{JobTitle}}Recipient's company / job title (when known)
{{SenderName}} {{SenderEmail}} {{SenderJobTitle}}The person who owns the email message
{{OrganizationName}}Organization Name from Settings
{{Today}}The send date
{{Record.column}}Any column of the related record, e.g. {{Record.subject}}
{{RecordName}} {{RecordUrl}}The related record's name and a link to open it
{{Signature}}Places your signature here; otherwise it is added at the end
{{RecordButton}}Places the Open record button here (emails about a record); otherwise it follows the message

Tokens work in both the subject and the body, and are case-sensitive.

Recipient lists

  • Create a Recipient List, then add List Members from its form.
  • A member can be a User, a Contact, a whole Dataverse Team (all members receive it) or an External Email.
  • Tick Opted Out to keep a member on the list without emailing them.
  • Duplicate addresses are sent once.

Notification rules (automatic emails)

  • Administration → Notification Rules → New: enter a table logical name (e.g. task, contact or a custom table). The form lists its email and lookup columns.
  • Choose When (created / updated), optional columns and a FetchXML condition, the template, and the recipients: record owner, created by, email columns, lookup columns, a list or fixed addresses.
  • Saving an active rule registers it automatically; the email is linked to the row that triggered it.

Signatures

Create your own signature under Signatures and tick My Default. It is pre-selected on every new email.
